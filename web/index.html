What this floor shows

These are saved run states and events, refreshed every two seconds. The duration comes from the run receipt; an active run shows elapsed wall time since its recorded start. Missing timings stay unknown.

The role cards describe responsibilities. Model-written plans and critiques are distinct from asset checks and the deterministic renderer. A role card is not evidence of a separate live model call; inspect the event messages and saved artifacts for what actually ran. Media generation may be skipped when existing assets meet the brief.

Rendering and technical checks do not establish perceptual approval. REVIEW_INCOMPLETE means required review evidence is missing. Review must refer to the exact current candidate file. This viewer cannot approve, publish, or change a run.
